Engineering Science, University of Toronto

I design, build and program mechatronic systems.

I'm Logan Riley, a first-year Engineering Science student at the University of Toronto. I'm passionate about robotics and mechatronics, and over the past two years I've designed and built a trading card sorter, a 3D-scanned interactive LED lamp and, with a friend, a Minecraft jukebox. My work has been recognized at regional and provincial science fairs, and I'm looking for opportunities to apply and extend these skills in research.

P.H.A.S.E.

A machine that sorts Magic: The Gathering cards. I built it alone over a calendar year: I modelled and printed the parts, designed and soldered the circuits, and wrote the software.

It identifies each card with perceptual hashing, looks up the price, and sorts the card into a high-value, low-value or unidentified box. Read how it works →.

550+hours of work
13,000lines of code, 5 languages
60unique 3D printed parts
110,000+card printings in its database
