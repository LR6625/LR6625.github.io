Activities

This page covers my life outside engineering: competitive golf and baseball, theatre and visual arts, and the leadership roles I held at school.

Sports

Golf

  • Junior Champion, Heriot Golf Club Tournament (2025). Won the overall competition against 36 players from across the Eastern Townships.
  • Regional tournaments, Eastern Townships Regional Golf Association (2024–2025). Competed in three a year, with fields of 36 players from across the region.
  • Junior Club Champion, Milby Golf Club (2024, 2025 and 2026). A three-peat, winning three years in a row.
  • MVP, high school varsity golf team (2024). The team’s best scorer that season, in my second year on the eight-player varsity team.
  • Course staff, Milby Golf Club. Managed the practice ranges, alongside coaching the junior golf camp.

Baseball

  • Regional Player of the Year, Sherbrooke Baseball Association, Midget (under 18) division (2025).
  • MVP, Midget A team (2025). Played shortstop, starting pitcher and second base, and led the team in hitting.
  • MVP, Bantam A team (2022). Pitched the opening game of the provincials.

Theatre, arts and writing

For two years I was part of the stage crew for my high school’s annual theatre production, involving 15 hours a month over five months. In one production, I held a role interacting with the crowd before the show and during the intermission.

I took the Visual Arts concentration for my last two years of high school, to build creative and artistic skills outside my usual comfort zone. Those skills have helped me come up with ideas for projects, including L’Odyssée de l’objet.

I also wrote for my school newspaper, the Piper Post, for two years, covering sports results and science topics and making holiday-themed parody videos.

Leadership

  • School Carnival Team Captain (March 2024). Won the vote to lead one of four teams across the school, 250 people each, and led the winning team.
  • Leader of the Opposition, Model Parliament (2022–2023). Led a nine-person caucus in writing and debating our platform.
  • Student Council Representative (2022–2024). A member for two years, in a generalist role working on multiple activities and theme days to make student life more enjoyable.
