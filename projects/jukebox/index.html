Minecraft jukebox

A real jukebox. Each 3D printed music disc carries an NFC chip, the same kind used in a credit card, that tells the jukebox which song to play.

ARKOpen source: 3D files, code, circuit diagram and shopping list on Mateo’s ARK page→

My part

This was a team project with my best friend Mateo (MateoTechLab on YouTube). I designed the internal structural components. Mateo and I wrote the code and built the circuit together, start to finish.

How it works

An Arduino Uno reads the disc's NFC chip, looks up which song it maps to and tells a DFPlayer Mini to play it from an SD card. A servo-driven mechanism lowers the disc for scanning and ejects it afterwards. There are status LEDs and a volume knob, and the whole circuit runs on a breadboard, so it's something people with no soldering experience can build.

Open source

The STL files, Arduino code, circuit diagram and a project overview are free to download on Mateo’s ARK page. Parts of the model started from designs by Fearless3DPrints and were modified to fit. Build-your-own kits are planned, with a waiting list, but aren't released yet.

Collaborating needed far more documentation than I was used to, and sharing a build with a friend I've known for more than ten years was worth it. The story behind it is in Mateo’s video.
