Projects

Three builds. I model parts in Blender, print them, wire the electronics and write the firmware and software myself.

2025 – Winter 2026

P.H.A.S.E. →

Perceptual Hashing for an Automated Sorting Engine: a machine that identifies and sorts Magic: The Gathering cards. Solo project, 550+ hours, finished as my Integrative Project in Science at Champlain. Gold Medal at the Estrie regional finals and the Ubisoft Prize at the provincial finals.

Late 2025 – early 2026

Minecraft jukebox →

A real jukebox where 3D printed music discs carry NFC chips. A team project with my friend Mateo. I designed the internal structure; we wrote the code and built the circuit together. Open source.

2025

3D-scanned interactive LED lamp →

A lamp sculpted from a 3D scan, with 50 addressable LEDs in the hair and a control page hosted by the lamp over its own Wi-Fi.
