Outreach

I love sharing what I build and how it works. I found robotics late, and looking ahead, I want to keep sharing it and help other people find it sooner than I did. This page covers what I've done so far: presenting P.H.A.S.E. at science fairs, the L’Odyssée de l’objet design competition, and my tutoring and coaching. The clubs and extracurriculars that aren’t about mentoring, teaching or sharing are on the Activities page.

  • Peer tutor, Champlain College Lennoxville (2025). Paid; sciences and English.
  • Private tutor (2023–2024). Math, chemistry, physics and English. I found three students by word of mouth.
  • Volunteer tutor. Two years in the adapted learning program, working one-on-one with a Grade 6–8 student each year, two students in all.
  • Junior golf camp coach (2023–2025). Three summers coaching groups of eight kids aged 7 to 14, combining practice sessions with playing on the course.

L’Odyssée de l’objet

A 48-hour industrial design competition for teams of three (November 2025). We designed a system to turn snow into drinking water for remote Canadian communities. The product had to work in theory, but the event itself involved building a 3D approximation of it. We pitched it to a jury in French.

Collegiate category awards

  • Gold Medal, Expo-sciences Estrie regional finals (March 2026). Best project among all collegiate presentations. This put P.H.A.S.E. on the Estrie delegation to the provincial finals.
  • Ubisoft Prize, Super Expo-sciences Hydro-Québec provincial finals (April 2026). Given to one engineering, robotics and software project at the senior or collegiate level.
  • CGI Prize for Engineering, Software and Robotics, regional finals (March 2026). Innovation through software integrated in a project.
  • Prix du Réseau ACTION TI, regional finals (March 2026). Best design and application of software at the collegiate level.

The Prix du Réseau ACTION TI also came with a nomination for the OCTAS Youth Prize in Information Technology.
